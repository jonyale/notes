Memory Breaks the Momentum Trade

The Invesco S&P 500 Momentum ETF is down 10.5% with six sessions left in July, its worst month since it launched in 2015, and four memory and storage names caused almost half of that. The S&P 500 sat 2% off its high through the whole thing. Three of the five shows took up the question, and they agreed on the facts while disagreeing about the cause. Animal Spirits puts it down to leverage and positioning. Mark Cuban points at how the AI buildout is financed. Aahan Menon spends an hour explaining why this is a bad week to hold a macro view, then states one anyway: if oil resumes its climb, the Fed hikes. Much of the back half of this issue depends on whether he is right.

Where volatility looks cheap

Protection here looks underpriced next to the size of move these speakers expect.

  • Short-dated interest rates Menon thinks the market is not positioned for a hike. Nobody else on this week's tape addressed it.
  • Oil A small share of the inflation basket that is currently swamping every other part of it.

Where volatility looks expensive

The move has already happened here, so protection is dear.

  • Individual memory and storage stocks After a 36% month, as SanDisk had, option sellers will have repriced. Nobody in these episodes discussed options; this is our read.
  • Korean chipmakers The forced selling has already run through them.

Glossary

high-bandwidth memory Memory packaged directly alongside a GPU's logic chip. Supplied by three companies and typically sold out a year ahead, which is why it behaves like a bottleneck.
backwardation A forward curve is in backwardation when longer contracts cost less per unit than short ones. In a rental market like GPUs that is the normal state when more supply is expected to arrive. Contango is the reverse, and in the GPU rental market it means providers no longer feel they need to discount to lock a customer in.
momentum stocks A systematic strategy that holds recent winners. It concentrates into whatever has led the market, so its composition can end up close to a single sector's.
dissaving Spending in excess of income, funded by savings or by asset gains. It reaches company revenue without a matching wage cost.
nominal GDP Output measured at current prices, combining real growth and inflation in a single number.
forward-deployed engineer An engineer placed inside a customer's organization to implement and adapt a vendor's technology. Both a revenue channel for the vendor and a measure of how much work deployment still takes.
DRAM Dynamic Random-Access Memory, the standard working memory in servers and PCs. High-bandwidth memory is a stacked, faster version of the same technology.
neocloud An operator that buys chips, secures power and rents capacity, without the wider software business of a traditional hyperscaler.
forward curve The set of prices for buying or renting something over different lengths of time. A one-month GPU contract and a one-year GPU contract sit at different points on the same curve.
